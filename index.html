Frequently Asked Questions

What are check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is available from 3:00 PM, and check-out is by 11:00 AM. Flexible timing can be arranged for early arrivals or late departures.

Is parking available?

Free street parking is available right outside the apartment. Additional garage parking can be arranged for an extra fee if needed.

Are pets welcome?

Small pets are welcome with prior approval. Please let us know in advance so we can ensure the apartment is prepared for your furry friend.

What's the maximum occupancy?

The apartment comfortably sleeps 2 adults, with the option to add a third guest using the portable bed. Perfect for couples or small families.

Can I cook in the apartment?

Yes! The fully equipped kitchen includes a stove, refrigerator, microwave, coffee maker, and all the essentials for preparing meals.

How far is the beach?

Just 250 meters (about 3 minutes walk) to Himarë's beautiful main beach with golden sands and calm, clear waters.
