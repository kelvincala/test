Pricing & Booking Policies

Pricing Information

  • Base Rate: €85/night for double occupancy
  • Additional Guest: €25/night (up to 3 total)
  • Taxes: 6% VAT included in all prices
  • Minimum Stay: 2 nights (3 nights peak season)

Payment Schedule

  • Deposit: 30% due at booking
  • Balance: Due 30 days before arrival
  • Peak Season: Full payment required
  • Cancellation: Refund per policy below

Cancellation Policy

  • 60+ days: Full refund minus €50 fee
  • 30-59 days: 50% refund
  • 14-29 days: 25% refund
  • <14 days: No refund

Modification Policy

  • Date Changes: Free up to 60 days prior
  • Guest Changes: Allowed until check-in
  • Early Check-in: €25 fee (subject to availability)
  • Late Check-out: €15/hour (max 4 hours)

Payment FAQ

When is payment due?

30% deposit is due immediately to secure your booking. The remaining balance is due 30 days before arrival, or immediately for last-minute bookings.

What currency do you accept?

All payments are processed in Euros (€). Your bank will handle currency conversion if needed. No additional fees from us.

What if I need to cancel?

Our flexible cancellation policy provides partial refunds depending on timing. Review the full policy above or contact us for special circumstances.

Is my payment secure?

Absolutely! We use bank-level SSL encryption and certified payment processors. Your information is never stored on our servers.

Questions About Payment?

We're here to help with any payment or booking questions. Contact us anytime!
